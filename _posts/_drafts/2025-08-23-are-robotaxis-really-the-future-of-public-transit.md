## Why Robotaxis?

Robotaxis are a hot topic for everyone nowadays with companies like Waymo expanding their services into new cities and new players entering the game like Zoox and Uber. The big question for many of us who want to build better cities are asking is why the investment in robotaxis? For many advocates of walkable cities that have finally made trains and buses normal, they don't see how robotaxis could possibly fix the problems of car dependent cultures like in the U.S.

I personally struggle with the tension between my excitement for technologies like autonomous vehicles, and my deep desire for designing cities we want to live in. We need public transit that gives people and the environment their city back from honking drivers who are tired of the traffic they're a part of. But can robotaxis really solve that problem as Silicon Valley companies would have us think? Today, the answer for me is *"It's complicated"*. I think if done right robotaxis can introduce a preferred mode of transportation that removes the burden of car ownership that so many people would rather not have. On the other hand, all those honking drivers might just get replaced by angry riders all complaining to support at the same time about the same traffic they were a part of before.

My issue with the later train of thought is that it is impossible to know whether that same behavior will truly repeat its self. Robotaxis introduce a fundamentally different form of transit compared to owning a car. It will require the adoption of new behaviors that don't fit the traditional model of car ownership. It's an open experiment where anything is possible if we take the chance to envision and build the way we would like it to make the places we live in better rather than the same status quo. This is a bit more optimistic take, but I'd rather begin designing these systems in a way that integrates well with daily life and the places we want to live in rather than complain later that it was terrible as we "expected" it to be. The only way we build a better future is by designing it ourselves. So, with that said, let's venture into how robotaxis could make public transit possible in car dependent nations like the U.S.
