## You Know Your City Needs Public Charging. Now What?

If you're reading this it's likely you know your city needs EV charging today or will sooner or later. You understand that the electrification train has left the station and that your city needs to prepare for the influx of battery electric vehicles (BEVs) on the road that will require proper infrastructure for the daily commuters, business fleets, or travelers passing through. Even if the majority of charging is done at home today, forward thinking cities recognize that there are community members who need charging beyond the home. Renters in apartments or even single-family homes need access to convenient Level 2 charging, whether on-site or at the places they park at regularly. Landlords may not be ready financially ready or EV literate enough to understand the needs of current residents desiring to go electric or prepare for future residents that are already plugged-in. Local business fleets may be in similar situations where they lack the EV know how to install the right EVSE at their fleet parking lots or understand the lifecycle cost savings that come with going electric without diminishing their business operations but rather supercharging them.

City leaders and community enthusiasts alike have the power to shift the narrative that people are not ready for electrification. By making the first move and establishing actionable plans and programs to invest in public EVSE, they can build a robust and widely available charging network that helps citizens see electrification is here, not just coming soon. With that said, let's venture into how to electrify your city through ubiquitous EV charging opportunities.
